# Saigon Reference

## Rule
Research real Saigon, not an abstract "Vietnamese aesthetic" and not AI-generated concept art as evidence.

## Reference categories
Collect and compare real photos/video for:
- road hierarchy and markings;
- motorbike flow and stopping behaviour;
- hẻm dimensions, entrances, turns, frontage, and visibility;
- shopfronts, awnings, shutters, signs, wires, poles, curb/sidewalk use;
- pickup/drop-off behaviour;
- raincoats, puddles, wet surfaces, drainage, glare/reflection;
- tree cover, materials, colour, lighting, density, and street clutter;
- everyday street activity and parked vehicles.

## Culturalization rule
Separate observation from design interpretation. Do not turn one photo, one district, one period, or one anecdote into a universal Saigon rule.

## Gameplay translation
For each observed trait ask:
1. Is it common/relevant enough to matter?
2. Can it change player behaviour or world readability?
3. What must be simplified for arcade play?
4. What would become stereotype or decorative noise if exaggerated?

## Authenticity test
Remove explicit city/country labels and Vietnamese copy. The remaining spatial logic, vehicle mix, street furniture, weather response, storefront rhythm, and traffic behaviour should still support the intended sense of place.

## Current gap
A systematic visual/reference pass has not yet been completed for the current low-poly city. Do not claim authenticity beyond what is actually researched and implemented.
