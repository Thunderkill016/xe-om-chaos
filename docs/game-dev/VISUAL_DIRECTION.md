# Visual Direction

## Goal
Avoid "generic low-poly city + Vietnamese text". The world should communicate place, gameplay, and tone through composition, shape, material, density, motion, signage, lighting, and street life.

## Art-direction method
Use a small set of validated art pillars, not a giant mood-board. A pillar is only accepted after real Saigon references and the current build support it.

## Candidate qualities to test — not final pillars
- motorbike-dominated
- lived-in rather than sterile
- warm/humid street atmosphere
- dense but readable
- playful chaos without visual noise

These are hypotheses, not cultural facts.

## Visual test
Hide:
- `SAIGON`
- `VIETNAM`
- all Vietnamese text

Then ask whether the scene still plausibly evokes Saigon. If not, identity is relying too heavily on labels.

## Gameplay readability
Art must help the player read:
- drivable surface versus solid mass;
- main road versus hẻm;
- openings and chokepoints;
- moving hazards;
- pickup/drop-off areas;
- weather/world-state changes.

A decorative object that creates ambiguous collision or hides traffic is a design problem, not polish.

## Current priority
Do not increase environmental density until collision truth and traffic readability are stable enough to support it.
